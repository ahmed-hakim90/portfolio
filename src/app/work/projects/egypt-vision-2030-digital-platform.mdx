## Overview

**رؤية مصر 2030** is a national Arabic **RTL** web product that explains Egypt’s digital transformation journey for citizens within the national vision framework. The home page connects three narrative pillars:

- **كنا فين** — traditional service experiences
- **هنروح فين** — the path toward unified government portals
- **إيه الجديد** — previews and initiatives

## Highlights

- **Dynamic ministry comparisons** — `/ministries/health`, `/ministries/agriculture`, and eight more ministries with reusable before/after UI
- **Project detail routes** — `/projects/moh-website`, `/projects/agriculture-2030` with loaders and per-route SEO
- **Health & quarantine case study** — mohp.gov.eg vs Nile Health; WLMS vs Cairo Quarantine portal — portfolio: [/work/nile-health-portal](/work/nile-health-portal)
- **Agriculture platform** — moa.gov.eg vs zra3a-2030 preview — portfolio: [/work/agriculture-2030-platform](/work/agriculture-2030-platform)
- **In-platform insights** — `/insights/digital-identity`, `/insights/automation-whatsapp`
- **Project gallery** — live demos + “عرض دراسة التطوير” CTAs
- **Pitch submissions** — “اقترح للعرض” via Formspree
- **SSR on Cloudflare Workers**

## Tech

React, TanStack Start, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Cloudflare Workers.

## Role & timeline

**Role:** Frontend developer · product architecture and content

Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_EGYPT_VISION_2030_DEMO_URL` in the portfolio environment (same value as `VITE_PLATFORM_DEMO_URL` in the platform repo). When configured, **View project** appears on the work listing and a live demo link on the case study page.
