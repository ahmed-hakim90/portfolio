## Overview

**Arkan Studio** is a bilingual digital flagship built around one idea: **Systems, Exposed**. Instead of presenting the studio as a gallery of flat screenshots, the experience explains how each product works through a Systems Atlas, project control rooms, and an interactive System X-Ray.

## Experience

- **Arabic and English** — Arabic-first routing, full RTL support, and localized content
- **Systems Atlas** — a browsable map of the studio's products and operational systems
- **Control Rooms** — project pages that reveal flows, layers, decisions, and outcomes
- **Project Builder** — an interactive intake that turns a visitor's brief into a live blueprint
- **Capabilities and approach** — service anatomy and a clear build sequence
- **Studio and team** — the six Arkan, team network, principles, and contact paths

## Content operations

The public experience is backed by a private Supabase CMS. The admin workspace manages projects, bilingual site copy, team profiles, media assets, brand settings, and incoming leads. Editors can publish projects, preview media paths, update lead status, and maintain the site without touching code.

## Engineering

Built with **Next.js 16**, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, next-intl, Framer Motion, Zod, and Supabase. Public intake endpoints include validation, honeypot protection, origin checks, rate limiting, and a guarded webhook path. The deployment also sets CSP, HSTS, frame protection, and a restrictive Permissions Policy.

## Outcome

Arkan turns a traditional agency portfolio into an interactive product narrative and gives the internal team a real operating layer for publishing work and following leads.
