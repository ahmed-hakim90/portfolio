## Overview

**Store OMS** is an operations dashboard for high-volume ecommerce: **orders**, **fulfillment status**, **shipments**, **tickets**, **warehouse**, and **analytics** in one place — with role-based areas for admins and day-to-day ops.

I contributed alongside a collaborator on **flows**, **dashboard UI**, and **integration-facing frontend work**, aligned with storefront and logistics tooling.
