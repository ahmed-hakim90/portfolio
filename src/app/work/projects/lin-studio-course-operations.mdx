## Context

**Lin Studio** is a bilingual mobile-first system for discovering Chinese courses and running the workflows behind enrollment.

## Problem

Course discovery, guest booking, payment proof, demand planning, and teacher coordination are often handled across messages and spreadsheets. Students lack a clear booking journey and operators lack one reliable view of demand and fulfillment.

## My role and solution

I built the product flows connecting public course discovery with guest booking, payment-proof review, group operations, demand analysis, and teacher-facing work. The design prioritizes mobile use and keeps bilingual navigation consistent across customer and admin contexts.

## Architecture and outcome

TanStack Start, React, TypeScript, and Supabase support a responsive frontend and production data model, with a safe local demo mode for development. The result is a compact example of turning an informal service workflow into a structured product without exposing student or payment data.
