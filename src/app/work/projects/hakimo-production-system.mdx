## Overview

Production-focused ERP for factories: real-time visibility into lines, costs per unit, and daily reporting — built with **React**, **TypeScript**, **Vite**, **Firebase**, **Zustand**, and **Tailwind CSS**.

## Scope

- Production lines with live status (active, stopped, maintenance)
- Daily reporting flows for workers and supervisors
- Role-based access and approval chains
- Cost analytics and dashboards aimed at answering “what does a unit actually cost?”
