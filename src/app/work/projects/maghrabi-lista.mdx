## Context

**MAGHRABI LISTA** is an internal catalog workspace for turning product documents and operational files into information teams can search, review, and export.

## Problem

Large product lists often arrive as PDFs and spreadsheets with inconsistent structure. Finding an item, validating its details, or preparing a clean export becomes slow and error-prone when every request starts with manual file reading.

## My role and solution

I built the application experience around fast catalog access: document ingestion, searchable records, structured review, spreadsheet exports, and AI-assisted processing where it provides practical value. The interface keeps long-running data tasks understandable and makes failure states recoverable.

## Architecture and outcome

Next.js and TypeScript provide the application layer, with Firebase for protected operational data and server-side document processing. The case study intentionally excludes private catalogs, credentials, and customer records while demonstrating how the product reduces repetitive catalog work.
