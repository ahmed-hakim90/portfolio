## Overview

This repo is my **dedicated portfolio / CV** codebase (`ahmed-portfolio`), separate from earlier learning demos like **Personal Site**. It focuses on a clean presentation of projects, experience, and contact — shipped as a fast static-friendly site.

The card image is a placeholder landscape asset; swap it for a real **hakimo-cv** screenshot under `public/` when you have one.
