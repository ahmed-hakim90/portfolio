## Overview

**Velora** is a multi-location point-of-sale and operations platform for cafés, restaurants, and retail businesses. It combines a touch-first cashier experience with the inventory, finance, customer, and administration tools required to run more than one branch.

## The challenge

Small and growing operators often combine a basic cashier, spreadsheets, disconnected inventory tools, and manual end-of-day reconciliation. This hides product cost, creates stock errors, and makes it difficult to compare branches or control permissions.

## What I built

- **Point of sale** — fast catalog, cart, customer attachment, split payments, held carts, receipts, and manager overrides
- **Cash sessions** — open and close shifts, cashier vault, reconciliation, and forced-close controls
- **Inventory** — per-warehouse stock, purchasing, transfers, waste, counts, batches, and landed cost
- **Recipes and costing** — ingredients, variants, recipe consumption, and margin visibility
- **Customers and loyalty** — profiles, order history, points earning and redemption
- **Finance** — sales invoices, expenses, chart of accounts, supplier payments, general ledger, and period closing
- **Online ordering** — public QR menu and first-party online orders
- **Administration** — organizations, branches, users, roles, feature flags, devices, audit logs, and platform console

## Engineering

Velora uses **Next.js 16**, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Zustand, Recharts, and Supabase PostgreSQL. Authentication, tenant boundaries, and granular permissions are enforced with row-level security and server-side guards. Zod-backed forms, automated database verification, unit tests, Playwright journeys, and a production gate support safer releases.

## Outcome

The result is one coherent operating system that follows a sale from the counter through stock, accounting, loyalty, and reporting—without forcing teams to stitch together separate tools.
