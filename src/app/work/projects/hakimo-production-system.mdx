## Overview

**HAKIMO ERP** is a manufacturing-focused ERP system for production teams that need clearer control over factory operations, daily reporting, HR workflows, approvals, dashboards, and cost visibility.

The product is built as a mobile-first RTL application using **React 19**, **TypeScript**, **Vite**, **Firebase**, **Zustand**, and **Tailwind CSS**. The goal is not just to display records; it is to help a factory team understand what is happening across production lines and make faster operational decisions.

## Problem

Factories often depend on fragmented spreadsheets, manual daily reports, and disconnected HR or approval processes. That makes it difficult to answer practical questions quickly:

- Which production lines are active, stopped, or under maintenance?
- What happened today across worker and supervisor reports?
- Who can approve or change sensitive operational data?
- How much does a production unit actually cost?
- Which KPIs should management watch every day?

## Solution

HAKIMO ERP brings those workflows into one structured system:

- Production lines with live status (active, stopped, maintenance)
- Daily reporting flows for workers and supervisors
- Role-based access and approval chains
- HR workflows connected to operational roles
- KPI dashboards for management visibility
- Cost analytics aimed at answering “what does a unit actually cost?”
- Mobile-first RTL interface for teams working from different devices

## Architecture

The frontend is organized around operational modules rather than generic pages. State is managed with **Zustand**, cloud data and authentication run through **Firebase**, and the interface is built with **Tailwind CSS** for fast iteration and responsive layouts.

The system is designed around role boundaries, production modules, reporting flows, and dashboard surfaces so each user sees tools that match their responsibility.

## Impact

The project demonstrates how frontend engineering can become operations software: a structured ERP product that turns production data, approvals, HR activity, and cost signals into a more usable business system.
