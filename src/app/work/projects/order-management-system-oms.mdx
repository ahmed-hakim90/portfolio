## Overview

**Store OMS** is an operations platform for e-commerce teams that need one workspace for order confirmation, fulfillment status, warehouse handoff, shipments, returns, tickets, role workflows, and analytics.

I contributed alongside a collaborator on **flows**, **dashboard UI**, and **integration-facing frontend work**, aligned with storefront and logistics tooling.

## Problem

E-commerce operations can become fragmented across WooCommerce, messaging tools, shipping dashboards, spreadsheets, and manual warehouse communication. That creates delays, unclear ownership, missed order states, and weak visibility for managers.

## Solution

Store OMS centralizes the operational lifecycle:

- Order lifecycle management from confirmation to fulfillment
- Warehouse workflows and handoff states
- Shipping integrations and delivery tracking patterns
- Returns management surfaces
- Role-based workflows for admins and operations teams
- Analytics dashboards for day-to-day visibility

## Architecture

The product connects commerce data, shipping workflows, WhatsApp-facing communication, and Firebase-backed operational state into one frontend workspace. Screens are designed around operational jobs rather than generic admin pages: orders, analytics, settings, tickets, warehouse, and fulfillment flows.

## Impact

Store OMS demonstrates a commerce operations layer that sits between the storefront and the team running the business. It turns order handling from scattered manual work into a clearer workflow-driven system.
